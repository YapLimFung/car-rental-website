Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our car rental services.

General Questions

1. How can I rent a vehicle?

Customers can rent a vehicle by selecting their preferred car, filling in the booking form, and providing the required information.

2. What documents are required for rental?

Customers need to provide a valid driving license and identification document before renting a vehicle.

3. How old must I be to rent a car?

Customers must meet the minimum age requirement and have a valid driving license to rent a vehicle.

4. Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Customers can cancel their booking according to the company's cancellation policy.

5. Can I extend my rental period?

Yes. Customers may request an extension by contacting our staff. Approval depends on vehicle availability.

Payment Questions

6. What payment methods are accepted?

We accept online banking, credit cards, debit cards, and other available payment methods.

7. When should payment be made?

Payment must be completed before the vehicle rental period begins.

8. Are there any additional charges?

Additional charges may apply for late returns, damages, or extra services requested by customers.

Vehicle Questions

9. Are all vehicles inspected before rental?

Yes. All vehicles are inspected and maintained regularly to ensure customer safety.

10. What happens if the vehicle breaks down?

Customers should contact our support team immediately for assistance and further instructions.

Still Have Questions?

If you need further assistance, please contact our staff team.
